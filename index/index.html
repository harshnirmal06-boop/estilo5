About us

Talent and events, finally under one roof

Estilo began with a simple observation: good talent often gets lost between the booking and the show. Models arrive without a clear brief, and events have strong ideas but no one steering them. We built an agency that handles both properly.

Because the same team casts the models and runs the event, nothing is lost in translation. You speak to us once, and we take care of the rest.
