Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose the models myself?

Yes. Share your brief and we will send a shortlist. You select the people you prefer and we confirm them.

How early should I get in touch?

As early as possible. Send us your date and requirements and we will confirm availability quickly.

Can you handle only part of an event?

Yes. Some clients book a single model or performer, while others hand us the complete event.

Do you work in other cities?

Please share the city and date, and we will confirm what is possible.

Planning a shoot, show or celebration?

Share your date and requirements and we will get back to you promptly.
